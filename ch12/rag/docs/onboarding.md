# Onboarding

## The First Week

On the first day, new team members get a laptop with Docker already installed. Access to the data
lake is requested on the first day and usually takes 2 working days.

## Your Buddy

Every new team member gets a buddy from the team for the first month. The buddy reviews your first
three pull requests.

## Learning

Each person has a learning budget of 1,500 euros a year for courses, books and conferences.
