# On-Call

## The Rotation

One engineer is on call each week. The rotation changes every Monday at 09:00. Swaps are allowed if
both people agree and the calendar is updated.

## Responding to Alerts

Acknowledge a page within 15 minutes. If you can't fix the problem within one hour, call the second
person on the rotation.

## After an Incident

Every incident gets a short written review within 3 working days. Reviews are blameless: we look for
what to change in the system, not who to blame.
